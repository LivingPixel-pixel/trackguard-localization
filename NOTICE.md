# Authorship and dependencies

TrackGuard is an authored research prototype developed during and after the SchubKI camera-localization challenge. The portable source adapts the team's local geometry and causal fusion implementation. Code provenance and the portability boundary are described in `docs/ENGINE.md`.

NumPy, OpenCV, Pillow and presentation-generation dependencies retain their own licenses. They are dependencies, not vendored source. The repository license covers the authored code, documentation and original synthetic graphics distributed here.

Partner datasets, recordings, calibration assets, measured per-frame trajectories, maps, labels, weights and caches are not included or licensed by this repository. The aggregate evaluation summary does not grant access to the underlying dataset.
